Minar Parents Privacy Policy

Controller: Sharifjon Mominov (Minar Academy), business address: 34 Mustaqillik Street, Bukhara, Uzbekistan; phone +998 90 512 21 80; email pythonsharif@icloud.com; website minaracademy.uz.

Data we process: parent — name, display name, phone number, login, password hash, optional profile photo, sessions, notifications; phone verification — number, status, attempts, IP address and time; student — name, class/group, schedule, attendance, grades, homework status, teacher feedback and contact details, certificates; billing — tuition fee, monthly charges, payment history, balance owed or prepaid, online-payment invoices (amount, provider, status, transaction ID and time).

Purposes: showing parents only the records of the student linked to them, securing sign-in, accepting and recording payments, sending notifications, and detecting errors and abuse.

Third parties: Payme, when online payment is enabled — payment happens on Payme’s page and we never receive card details; we send Payme the invoice number and amount, and Payme sends us the transaction ID, time and status; a balance is cleared only after Payme’s server confirms the payment. Telegram Gateway (Telegram) receives the phone number to deliver verification codes. We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

Security: HTTPS everywhere, hashed passwords, short-lived rotating session tokens, sign-in rate limits, and a server-side check that the student is linked to the requesting parent.

Retention: account data is kept while the account is active; student academic and billing records are kept as long as needed for education services and applicable accounting obligations; database backups rotate automatically and are deleted when their retention period ends. Contact us for specific periods.

Account deletion: in the app settings, processed immediately, or by request using the contacts above. We delete the login and password, all sessions, name, phone number, photo, notifications, phone-verification records and the link to the student. Online-payment invoices, if any, are kept for accounting with the parent record de-identified. The student’s academic and billing records are not deleted because they belong to the academy. Copies in backups are removed when the backup retention period ends.

Your rights: view your data, edit your profile and delete your account in the app; request corrections or ask questions using the contacts above. The app is for parents; students do not use it. When this policy changes, the date on this page is updated.
